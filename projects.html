UnderGround

About the Project

This game was developed as part of the discipline "Introduction to Computing". It consists of a 2D story based game made in Bitsy, where the player controls a worker and tries to find what is wrong with the Train he finds himself trapped in.


Technologies Used

  • Bitsy Color
  • Pixilart (Pixel Art)

Key Learnings

During the development, I learned all the basics of how a game works and the level of cooperation the whole team needs to have in order to make it work.
